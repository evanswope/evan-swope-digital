1. Space Cadet Layout

4 Flippers (2 lower + 2 upper midfield) surround an open central arena with the 16-LED Mission Ring & 7-LED Rank Ring. Shoot the Left Launch Ramp to start missions & claim promotions, or the Right Hyperspace Chute for jackpots & Gravity Well!

2. Vowel & Consonant Zones

Consonants live on the perimeter: Left Drop Bank [B][P][M], Mission Standups [T][D][N], Right Drop Bank [K][G][S], and 3 Wormhole Kickers [L][R][S]. Vowels live at the top: 3 Rollover Lanes /i/ /ɑ/ /u/ & 4 Attack Bumpers /ɛ/ /ɪ/ /oʊ/ /ʌ/.

3. Missions & Promotions

Hit [T][D][N] to choose a mission, then shoot the Launch Ramp. Completing missions lights the Inner 7-Star Rank Circle. When all 7 Rank Lights are lit, shoot the Launch Ramp to ascend to the next Ziggurat Tier!
