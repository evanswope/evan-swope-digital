Apparel B2B & Distribution

// Strategist Notes

Challenge: How do you rapidly scale wholesale distribution and secure national retail partners without destroying bottom-line margins or causing operational chaos?

// Marx Companies

Positioning 6 Distinct Beef Programs

6 Specialty Beef Programs

// Strategist Notes

Challenge: How do you sell 6 different premium beef programs without them cannibalizing each other in an intensely crowded commodity market?

Approach: Crafted unique brand positioning and visual prestige for each program (*Irish Nature Beef*, *Ocean Beef*, *Pure Black Wagyu*, etc.) across 5 distinct axes: feed, country of origin, age, breed, and price point.

Learnings: Generated $10.4M in program revenue growth over two years (a 48% increase). When you clearly articulate the unique pedigree of a product across multiple dimensions, you turn a commodity into an indispensable menu feature.

Test Kitchen Operations

// Strategist Notes

Challenge: Directing a high-pressure, greasy, 8-hour kitchen shoot involving a matrixed team (CEO, guest chefs, shipping, videographers, copywriters, and social assistants) without bottlenecks or product waste.

Approach: Became sole manager of test kitchen productions, engineering a synchronized studio schedule and aligning every stakeholder on exact deliverables, shot lists, and brand goals before the burners turned on.

Learnings: Increased usable A-roll output by 10x (from ~20 to 200+ elite shots per shoot) while eliminating downtime. True creative scaling isn't just about camera gear; it's about flawless operational choreography.

Social Media & Momentum
